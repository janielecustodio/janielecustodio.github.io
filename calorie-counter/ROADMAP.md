# Roadmap

Everything that was tracked here has shipped: edit entry, log again,
recent/frequent foods, copy day/meal, PWA installability, micronutrients,
manual quick-add, custom meals/recipes, a Trends view, water/weight
logging, and targets/goals.

Streaks and social/sharing features were explicitly ruled out — this is
a private single-user app and stays that way. CSV export was also
explicitly ruled out.

Add new ideas here as they come up.
